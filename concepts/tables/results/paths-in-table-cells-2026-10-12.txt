2. Break after `/` with a mark: a plain page, a 150 px box, the path
   /var/vcap/store/JOB with U+00AD after every `/` but the first,
   code.path { hyphens: manual; hyphenate-character: "\21B5" }.
   - Chrome, WebKit: break at a `/`, the mark shows.
   - Firefox: breaks after the first `/` and after store/, its own break
     opportunities, with no mark (the same as without the soft hyphens).
   - Selecting the path gives the U+00AD in all three. Copying it in
     Chrome (Cmd+C, clipboard read back): "/var/­vcap/­store/­JOB",
     3 soft hyphens.
